# Debate Room

A remote app for small friend groups to stage their own Jubilee-style discussion episodes, running structured **Rounds** in formats like Spectrum, Middle Ground and Surrounded.

## People

**Host**:
The person who creates a **Room**, chooses its **Prompts** and runs its **Rounds**. A Host may also choose to take part as a **Participant**.
_Avoid_: Admin, moderator, owner

**Participant**:
A person in a **Room** who takes part in its **Rounds**, identified only by a nickname they choose when joining. The **Host** sets how many a Room allows.
_Avoid_: Player, user, guest, member

**Spectator**:
A person in a **Room** who watches the **Stage** and may react, but never takes part in **Rounds**. A Spectator may switch to **Participant** between Rounds if there is room.
_Avoid_: Audience, viewer, observer

**Center**:
In a Surrounded **Round**, the **Participant** who defends the **Prompts** against everyone else.
_Avoid_: Defender, hot seat

**Challenger**:
In a Surrounded **Round**, any **Participant** other than the **Center**, who may take the chair to argue against the Center.
_Avoid_: Opponent, attacker

**Side**:
In a Middle Ground **Round**, one of two named groups (e.g. "Cat people / Dog people") that each **Participant** belongs to for the whole Round.
_Avoid_: Team, group, camp

## Sessions

**Room**:
A single gathering of a **Host**, **Participants** and **Spectators**, joined by invite link or QR code, which holds a **Lineup**. A Room moves through three phases: **Planning**, **Live** and **Ended**.
_Avoid_: Game, session, lobby, episode

**Planning**:
The phase of a **Room** before it opens, when the **Host** builds the **Lineup** and others can only see the **Preview**.

**Live**:
The phase of a **Room** when people can join and **Rounds** run.

**Ended**:
The phase of a **Room** after the **Host** closes it or it expires from inactivity.

**Preview**:
The read-only view of a **Room**'s **Lineup** that anyone with the invite link sees before the Room is **Live**, with **Surprise Prompts** hidden.
_Avoid_: Agenda, teaser

**Waiting Room**:
The state of a **Room** between **Rounds** (and before the first), where **Participants** gather while the **Host** sets up what comes next.
_Avoid_: Lobby

**Lineup**:
The ordered list of **Rounds** a **Room** will run, planned by the **Host** and editable at any time.
_Avoid_: Playlist, schedule, agenda

**Round**:
One stretch of discussion in a **Room**, run in a single **Format** over one or more **Prompts**.
_Avoid_: Game, segment, episode

**Stage**:
The shared live view of the current **Round** that everyone in the **Room** sees.
_Avoid_: Board, screen, display

**Floor**:
Who is currently speaking. One or more people "have the Floor" when the **Host** (or the **Format**'s rules) gives them their turn; a **Participant** can raise a hand to ask for it.
_Avoid_: Mic, turn, spotlight

**Format**:
The set of rules that shapes a **Round**: Spectrum, Middle Ground or Surrounded.
_Avoid_: Mode, game type

## Content

**Topic**:
A theme that groups related **Packs**, such as "Technology" or "Relationships".
_Avoid_: Category, subject

**Pack**:
A curated set of **Prompts** under one **Topic**. A **Featured Pack** ships with the app; a **Host** can also write custom **Prompts**.
_Avoid_: Deck, question set

**Featured Pack**:
A **Pack** that ships with the app.
_Avoid_: Default pack, built-in pack

**Prompt**:
A statement that a **Round** is about, phrased so people can take a position on it (e.g. "Social media does more harm than good").
_Avoid_: Question, topic, statement

**Surprise Prompt**:
A **Prompt** the **Host** has marked to stay hidden in the **Preview** until it is played.
_Avoid_: Secret prompt, hidden prompt
