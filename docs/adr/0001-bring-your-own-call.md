# Conversation happens on the group's own call, not in the app

Debate Room carries no audio, video or chat in v1. Groups talk on a call they already use (typically Discord), and the app is only the staging layer: the **Stage**, the **Floor**, positions, votes and timers. Building in WebRTC video/voice would cost far more and duplicate what friend groups already have. As a result the app never hears what is said, so v1 keeps a **Recap** of events rather than a spoken transcript; a future transcript or built-in voice should plug into the **Floor** concept rather than replace it.
