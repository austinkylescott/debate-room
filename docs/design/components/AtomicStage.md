# AtomicStage

The Spectrum Stage after the reveal: the Prompt in a breathing orange amoeba and the Floor in a breathing teal kidney, each over a layer of slowly drifting small shapes, with the Spectrum drawn as an orbit.

**Layers in each container:**
1. Drifting shapes (sparkle, starburst, boomerang, leaf, diamond, dots) peek out from behind the container.
2. The container shape, whose edges morph between three variants of the same path.
3. Content, in a fixed inner area that never moves.

**Orbit:**
- **Notches:** five notches on an arc around a slowly turning atom. Each notch label carries its count.
- **Electrons:** people, with initials, the pole fill, a Mood emoji, and a numbered sky diamond for a raised hand. The speaker wears an orange ring.
- **Your orbit:** open, with name pills and a compass star. Tapping another orbit opens it and closes yours.
- **No separate key.** Every fact sits on the orbit itself.

**Floor:**
- An atom timer: an orange draining arc with an ink electron at its tip.
- The raised-hand queue rides the timer's outer orbit as small electrons.
- Raise hand is the spark-orange button.

**Accessibility:**
- The orbit has an accessible name that summarises every count.
- Text never sits on a moving edge.
- All ambient motion and the morph stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The SMIL animations are not added at all.
