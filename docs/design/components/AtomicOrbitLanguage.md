# AtomicOrbitLanguage

How the atom metaphor extends across the app: a nucleus is what people gather around, electrons are people, and the orbit you're in is the open one.

| Where | Nucleus | Electrons and orbits |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting Room | The Room code | Arrivals fly in. The Host wears the orange ring. Spectators sit on the dashed outer shell. |
| Spectrum | Each notch | Your orbit is open with names. Crossing a notch is a quantum jump. |
| Middle Ground | Each Side (two atoms) | Stepping in moves an electron into the shared bond, the avocado bowl. |
| Surrounded | The Center | The chair is the inner orbit, joined by an orange bond. Challengers wait on the outer shell. Vote diamonds fling a challenger out. |
| The Floor | The speaker | Their time is a draining orange orbit. Raised hands queue as numbered diamonds. |
| Mood Check-in | The 15-second countdown | Moods orbit it, and the most common one swells. |
| Lineup and menus | Each Round (an atom in a chain) | The live Round is orange with a circling electron. Menus open as an orbit of actions around your avatar. |
| Recap | Each Prompt | Electrons show the split. The night is a molecule, and the most divided Prompt gets a sparkle. |
